Features

🚀

One-Click Save

Click the extension icon to instantly send the current page as a beautifully formatted Markdown link.

📋

Right-Click Menu

Send selected text, links, or entire pages through the convenient context menu integration.

✨

Clean Format

Auto-formats content as [Title](URL) in Markdown with link preview disabled for a tidy channel.

🔒

Privacy First

All credentials stored locally. No data collection, no tracking, no external servers.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Instant delivery to your Telegram chat. No delays, no loading screens, just quick saves.

🎯

Flexible Targets

Send to any channel, group, or private chat. Support for both Chat IDs and @usernames.

How It Works

1

Create a Telegram Bot

Message @BotFather on Telegram, send /newbot, and follow the prompts to get your Bot Token.

2

Get Your Chat ID

Use @userinfobot or the getUpdates API to find your channel/group Chat ID, or simply use @channelname.

3

Configure the Extension

Enter your Bot Token and Chat ID in the extension settings. Your credentials are stored securely in your browser.

4

Start Saving

Click the extension icon or use the right-click menu to instantly save any web content to Telegram!

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

TL;DR: Telegram Saver operates entirely locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to external servers (except to Telegram's API when you explicitly send a message).

Introduction

Welcome to Telegram Saver. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our browser extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Telegram Saver is designed with privacy as a core principle. The extension:

  • Does not collect personal information - We do not gather any identifiable information about you
  • Does not track your browsing history - Your web activity remains completely private
  • Does not use analytics or tracking tools - No third-party services monitor your usage
  • Processes data locally only - All operations happen within your browser

How We Use Information

The extension accesses the following data solely for its core functionality:

Page Information

When you click "Send to Telegram", the extension reads the current page's title and URL to format the message. This data is sent directly to Telegram's API and is not stored or processed elsewhere.

Local Storage

The extension uses browser local storage to save your Bot Token and Chat ID. This data never leaves your device except when making API calls to Telegram, and is stored securely within your browser's sandboxed environment.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We want to be crystal clear:

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not share your data with third parties
  • We do not have servers that receive your data
  • No external analytics or advertising services are integrated

The only external communication is with Telegram's official API (api.telegram.org) when you explicitly choose to send a message.

Permissions Explained

Telegram Saver requires certain browser permissions to function. Here's why:

activeTab

Allows access to the current tab's URL and title when you click the extension icon or use the context menu. This permission only activates on user interaction.

storage

Required to save your Bot Token and Chat ID locally in your browser. This data is stored in Chrome's secure local storage and never transmitted to our servers.

contextMenus

Enables the right-click menu option "Send to Telegram" for quick access to the extension's functionality.

scripting

Required to extract the page title from the active tab. This permission is used minimally and only when you initiate a send action.

Data Security

All data processed by Telegram Saver remains within the secure, sandboxed environment of your browser. We follow browser extension security best practices to ensure:

  • No unauthorized access to your data
  • Isolation from other extensions and web pages
  • Compliance with browser security policies
  • Secure transmission to Telegram via HTTPS

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over the extension:

  • Disable or Remove: You can disable or uninstall the extension at any time through your browser settings
  • Clear Data: You can clear any locally stored credentials by removing the extension or clearing extension data
  • Transparency: The extension's behavior can be inspected through browser developer tools

Children's Privacy

Telegram Saver is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we don't collect any personal information at all, this risk is inherently minimized.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the extension or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Open Source and Transparency

We believe in transparency. The extension's code can be inspected through your browser's extension management interface, allowing technically inclined users to verify our privacy claims.
